How are these figures calculated?

Why was Min / Occurrence removed?

Min / Occurrence was a manual assumption about how many minutes an issue “costs”. It was not historical time-log data and it is not needed for your team’s calculation, so it has been removed. Leaving it at 1 would have changed the results; it is no longer used anywhere.

Do we need historical time logs?

No. Enter only the number of issues recorded. The dashboard uses the fixed time of play: 100 minutes per match and 9 matches per round.

Issues per round and minute

Issues per round = total issues ÷ number of rounds. Issues per minute = total issues ÷ total minutes of play. For several rounds, total minutes are rounds × 900.

How is impact average calculated?

For each round: round issues ÷ 900 minutes × 100. Across several equally sized rounds, the average equals combined issues ÷ (number of rounds × 900) × 100.

Are the example minute figures used?

No. Values such as Feed 2.0 minutes or VAR 2.5 minutes are not used because this dashboard measures issue frequency against time of play, not assumed minutes per occurrence.
